Lifelike AI art, animated: three ways

All three methods use the same AI-generated, fictional character. The body is one side-view image cut into parts. The face is one portrait, plus AI-made expression and eyes-closed versions of it. Everything renders live in WebGL. Turn on debug to see the bones, the mesh and the masks.

Body: gameplay sprite

A. Cutout parts on a skeletonRigid pieces rotate at the joints, with painted fills where the arm hid the jacket
B. Skinned mesh (Live2D-style)Whole-limb textures bend at the joints, plus breathing and hair sway
C. AI-generated frames6 walk poses from a second AI image, played as a flipbook

Face: the expression "gambit" (flow morph)

One AI portrait plus 10 AI versions of the same character: happy, angry, sad, surprised, distressed, winded, yelling, projecting and pain. The versions are aligned and morphed with optical flow. Blinks, eye movement and breathing are procedural. F2 (flow morph) is the default; F1, F3 and F4 stay selectable in the view menu. Distracted is eyes only: the gaze wanders around the room while the head keeps its idle sway. The head-turn versions were rejected and are only in the "rejected" view.
