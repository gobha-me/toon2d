# Pitfalls: symptom → cause → fix → how to prove it

| Pitfall | Symptom | Cause | Fix | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foot skate** | Planted foot slides, moonwalks, or feet "ice-skate" at speed changes | Gait phase driven by time; IK can't reach (hip too high); held timing applied to feet; crossfade blends a planted foot | Phase by distance, cycle = 2 × stride; world-space foot locks; hip ≈ 0.92 × leg; feet + IK every tick even when the upper body is on 2s/3s | `feet.slip` mean < 0.35, p95 < 1.0 px/tick; `feet.planted` with 0 "planted while floating" |
| **Planted in mid-air** | Foot locked while airborne after a jump→land crossfade; hangs below/above floor | Lock taken at a blended, off-ground position | Never lock in the air: snap to ground before locking; snap step targets to ground | `feet.planted` floating = 0 |
| **See-through gaps** | Background shows between thigh and shin, shoulder and torso at bent angles | Parts end exactly at the pivot; box cuts; bone len ≠ sprite pivot→tip | Overlap 2-3 px past each joint, round caps/discs, len from markers | `gaps.connected` (one main blob ≥ 97%), `gaps.joints_covered` ≥ 95% |
| **Hinge joints** | Elbows/knees fold like a paper doll; visible corner at the pivot | Two rigid rectangles meeting at a point | Single-path capped strokes for limbs, or a joint cap part; slight bulge on the inside of the bend | Rubric "puppet"; zoom on `sheet-run.png` knees |
| **Box-cut parts** | A slab of coat/torso swings with the arm | The cut polygon included neighbouring cloth | Cut along the true silhouette + caps; fill what the arm covered on the torso. Drawn in-betweens only win if they match the source rendering style | Before/after GIF of run extremes |
| **Rigid coat tube** | Long coat/skirt reads as a stiff tube; row-shift fakes only partly help | One rigid part | Split into 2-3 chained parts on springs (`createChain`), drive from hip acceleration; keep its silhouette from the torso. **Also drag by speed**: rest angle leans back with |vx| (≈ 0° standing → 30-45° at full run) or apply wind = −vx·k, else it hangs like a banner while running (cold run G2 passed every check this way) | `sheet-runjump.png`: coat lags on start/stop/landing; `sheet-run.png`: hem trails behind the back leg at speed |
| **Popping turns** | Character flips instantly (`scale.x = -1`) | No turn state | Turn state ≥ 4 ticks: head first, chest, hips; narrow upper body at the flip (kick); swap near/far legs draw order | `turn.not_instant` 4-30 ticks; `sheet-turn.png` |
| **Weak pixel faces** | Face unreadable at game scale; mush | Vector face downsampled; face drawn before quantize; anchor off the skin | Hand-placed pixel clusters after quantize; exaggerate; colour marks; anchor = centre of the eye-mouth box, ≥ 4 px inside the front edge; dialogue bust for real acting | `face.expressions` (pixel diff ≥ 4%); `expressions.png` |
| **Filter blur** | Soft, smeared pixels; uneven pixel sizes | CSS scaling, non-integer scale, smoothing on, canvas size ≠ CSS size × dpr | Integer k, canvas pixels exact, CSS = px/dpr, `image-rendering: pixelated`, smoothing off on every context | `pixel.no_filter_blur` (screen vs nearest upscale ≥ 98% match) |
| **Single side-view parts** | The top remaining puppet tell after everything else is fixed: turns and 3/4 moments look flat | One drawing per part | Draw 3/4 and front versions of head and torso; swap by yaw; at least for the head | Rubric "puppet" on turns |
| **Expression crossfades** | Ghost double mouths/brows during changes | Linear blend between presets | Takes: anticipation (squint/dip) → fast move with slight overshoot → settle (starter reactions: `anticipate`, `overshoot`, `settle`); snap shapes at pixel scale | Watch `expressions.gif`; rubric "face" |
| **Choppy 3s** | Run looks stuttery | Upper body held on 3s | 2s is the sweet spot for runs; 1s for smooth mode; 3s only for stylised idles | `tech-twos` comparison, rubric "motion" |
| **Smears on runs** | Run looks blurry/glitchy | Smear frames used for locomotion | Smears only on attacks and dashes (and big jumps at most) | Rubric "readability" |
| **AI flipbook locomotion** | "4 legs", scale pumping, popping, 6 fps | AI-generated walk frames used directly | Never for gameplay locomotion; rig it, render 3D, or hand-draw over AI keys | n/a: don't ship it |
| **Lifelike body via cutout/mesh** | Baked lighting rotates with limbs; rubber knees; banana pockets | Photographic texture on deforming/rotating parts | Use lifelike art for busts only (flow-morph face); stylise or 3D-render gameplay bodies | Rubric "uncanny/appeal" |
| **Mirrored, synchronous motion** | Arms/legs perfectly mirrored, everything on one clock | Same curve for both sides | Asymmetry (different timing/amplitude per side), overlap springs, noise in idle | `idle.alive`, `antipuppet.toggle` |
| **Dead idle** | Character freezes when standing | No idle layer | Breath + weight shift + noise sway + blinks + occasional glance | `idle.alive` (mean frame diff ≥ 1%, ≥ 30% distinct frames) |
| **Angle wrap bug** | Limb spins 360° for a frame when angle crosses ±180° | Lerp/snap on unwrapped angles | `wrapAngle` before snapping; `lerpAngle` for blending | `determinism`, eyeball `sheet-turn.png` |
| **Markers rendered** | Magenta/cyan dots on the character | Marker pixels left in the sheet | Use `sheet.clean.png` from `sheet-markers.mjs` | `expressions.png`, any sheet |
| **Palette remap** | Sprite colours change hue in pixel mode | Quantizer palette lacks the sheet's colours | parts.json `palette` (auto) or add colours to `rig.palette` | compare `sheet-idle.png` to the sheet |
| **Seams in silhouette test** | Light lines between parts when the character is filled black | Anti-aliased part edges, overlaps too small | Quantize alpha, overlap, outline parts | Fill the `character` layer solid and inspect |
| **Arm crosses the face in the air** | During jump/fall the near hand or glove covers eyes/forehead; face unreadable for the whole airborne arc | Airborne arm pose copied from a run extreme, or IK/FK targets near the head; near arm drawn over the head | Airborne arms go out/back and below eye line (near arm low and outward, far arm raised behind); draw order keeps the head over the far arm; check every jump cell | `sheet-jump.png`/`sheet-runjump.png`: both eyes + mouth visible in every airborne cell (cold run G1 baseline failed this at 15/15 score) |
| **Face overflows the skin patch** | Mouth sits on the chin edge/neck, brows in the hair; expressions look pasted on | Face template sized for the starter head, not the sheet's skin area | Measure the skin box on the head part (sheet-markers prints `faceAnchor`); fit eyes in the top half, mouth ≥ 1 px above the chin edge; shrink extremes (surprised/hurt) to stay inside | `expressions.png`: every cell's features inside the skin |
| **Merged dark legs** | At 1x the two trouser legs read as one blob in passing poses | Same dark value on near and far leg, no gap or rim | Far leg one shade darker (0.72 shade) AND near leg gets a 1 px light rim/outline break; widen the passing-pose knee gap by 1 px. Keep the backdrop's value away from the character's darkest tones (a mid/light backdrop fixed G1 readability 2 → 3) | Rubric "readability"; native-size `sheet-run.png` cells at passing |
| **Lip doubling (flow morph)** | Two upper lips mid-change | Key sampled at `q + (1−w)B` (sign error) | Chain-consistent sampling `q + Σ w_j B_j − B_i` | Step the weight 0→1 in 0.1 steps, inspect mouth crops |
| **Ghost teeth when talking** | Teeth/tongue fade in and out through closed lips | Mouth shapes crossfaded or flow-morphed per syllable | No proven fix yet: the F4 hybrid (local jaw warp + real teeth, hard-switched mouths) was tried and mostly rejected by the owner. Keep dialogue mouths minimal on F2, prototype, get a human side-by-side | Owner ranking: talking F1 > F2 (ghosts) > F3 (fails); F4 mostly rejected |
| **Hair masked out / hair drift** | Hair frozen while the face moves, or hair changes style on head turns | Morph mask excludes hair (AI versions disagree on hair) | Give hair its own springs/mesh motion; don't use AI head-turn versions | Watch the hairline in the bust GIF |
| **Head-turn images for 'distracted'** | Turn reads as a different picture; hair/identity drift; wrong angle | AI turned versions can't hold identity or angle | Eyes-only gaze wandering around the room (saccades, holds, returns), no new art | Owner review: head-turn distracted doesn't work |
| **No take-off crouch** | Jump leaves the ground with no anticipation (jump 1→2 cells identical stance) | Physics launches on the press tick; pose blends straight to `jumpPose` | **Built into the starter (0.1.0):** 4–6 tick jump-squat at walk/stand (fewer at full run) before the impulse (`physics.js` squatWalk/squatRun + `crouchPose`), with a visible hip drop/knee bend/lean and stretch on release; jump buffer kept so presses still feel responsive. Custom rigs: keep the same pattern | `sheet-jump.png` cell 1–2 shows a crouch |
| **Dive take-off** | Launch frames lean the torso 40–45° forward; the jump reads as a dive. Shallow crouch and short run strides often come with it | Custom jump/crouch pose adds forward spine pitch on top of the crouch lean, or the lean is never unwound in the air | Keep crouch lean 5–30° and unwind it to ≤ 15° within ~8 airborne ticks (the starter's `crouchPose` → `jumpPose` blend does this). Hip drop ≥ 15 % of standing hip height. Verify with `scripts/takeoff.mjs` | `sheet-jump.png` / `sheet-runjump.png` cells around the launch; `takeoff.mjs` `launch_lean` / `launch_unwind` |
| **Face slides off the head on turns** | Mid-turn the eyes/mouth drift onto the hair or the back of the head | Face yaw offset larger than the skin patch; features moved but hair mass is a fixed sprite | **Built into the starter (0.1.0):** `face-clamp.js` clamps every feature to the head ellipse, caps yaw parallax, and at peak turn draws only the near eye + a 1 px mouth. Still better long-term: drawn 3/4 head parts | `sheet-turn.png` cells 5–7 stay on the skin |

| **AI still with no sidecar** | Cannot prove model/date/prompt; license audit fails | Prompt only in chat history | Save `<name>.gen.json` next to every AI still (prompt, tool, model, date UTC); list in PROVENANCE.md; never invent lost prompts | New `src/*.jpg` always accompanied by `.gen.json` |
